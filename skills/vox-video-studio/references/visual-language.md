# Clean VOX paper-collage language

Derive topic, voice, palette, typography, and exclusions from the confirmed content profile. Keep the shared VOX grammar:

- large clean paper planes and controlled torn edges;
- one focal subject, one supporting action, and one environmental detail per scene;
- explicit foreground/subject/background depth;
- maps, process chains, cross-sections, exploded diagrams, counters, and failure states;
- one meaningful visual change roughly every 1.5–2.5 seconds;
- subtitles and factual labels rendered in Remotion, not baked into generated scene assets.

Append this quality requirement to every prompt:

> Clean high-definition illustration, refined materials, pure controlled colors, crisp edges, spotless background, even lighting, and a polished editorial finish. Avoid grain, noise, dust, stains, dirty texture, mottled spots, film grain, compression artifacts, pixelation, oversharpening, distressed aging, random fibers, excessive fragments, visual clutter, watermark, logo, and unintended text.

Generate cutouts on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background with full silhouette, generous padding, no cast shadow, no floor plane, no reflection, and no key green inside the subject.

Keep routes behind people and major props. Keep annotations away from faces, hands, and readable objects. Prefer restrained crossfades or motivated hard cuts. Do not use random tape, masks, lines, dust, constant zoom, or meaningless shake.
