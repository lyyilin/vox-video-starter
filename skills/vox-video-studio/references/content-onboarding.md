# Content direction onboarding

Run this before producing the first episode.

## Round 1: identity and audience

Ask:

1. What is the channel about in one sentence, and which topics should it repeatedly cover?
2. Who is the primary viewer, what do they already know, and what should they feel or understand after watching?
3. Which platforms, aspect ratio, and normal duration should the workflow target?

## Round 2: editorial and creative constraints

Ask:

1. What evidence standard is required, and which claims or sources are unacceptable?
2. What narration personality, spoken/written balance, and forbidden expressions should be used?
3. What visual palette, illustration style, information graphics, and visual exclusions should be used?

Ask follow-ups only for decisions that materially change production. Then write `studio/config/content-profile.md` using `studio/config/content-profile.example.md` as the shape.

Show the full profile and ask for the configured direction-confirmation phrase. Record the approval and date in the profile. If the user changes direction later, update the profile and request confirmation again.

Do not bake a public sample account into the profile. Samples illustrate fields only.
